## Airwindows Acceleration2 and ToTape8

`native/airwindows/airwindows_bridge.cpp` and `native/airwindows/totape8.cpp`
contain standalone extractions of the Airwindows Acceleration2 and ToTape8
double-precision processing algorithms from commit
`781eaee378303c7dc4d9edcaabb086cf160ff5df`. The VST host, SDK, GUI and preset
code are not included.
